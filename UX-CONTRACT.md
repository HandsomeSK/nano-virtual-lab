## Page indices and appreciation

The shared fragment manager opens collapsed ancestors, focuses section targets, preserves URL/query/history state and publishes a synthetic hash notification after a real same-document URL change; that state notification never causes a second scroll.

## Unified 3D Lab

The 18 October 2026 brief supersedes the previous split between Lab, Device and the interface article. All interactive scientific WebGL models belong to `/lab`, in four continuous modules: Graphene atomic structure (`#graphene`), MoS₂ atomic structure (`#mos2`), MoS₂ transistor (`#transistor`) and Interface engineering (`#interface-engineering`). All four modules and their controls are directly present in the page; reaching another model requires only scrolling. There are no model-category tabs, hidden whole-model accordions, secondary model routes or navigation-only substitutes for a viewer.

Each AtomicWorkbench and DeviceViewer instance owns its own state and uniquely identified controls. Changing selection, visibility, camera, layer configuration or Reset in one module does not alter another. Graphene and MoS₂ are fixed-material instances; their detail, layer and stacking controls remain local. Legacy material query URLs still render the complete Lab without hiding or reordering either material. On initial entry or an explicit cross-route commit, `/lab?material=graphene` or `/lab?material=mos2` without a fragment gains the matching `#graphene` or `#mos2` fragment and focuses that existing section. `history.replaceState` preserves all query parameters and history state without adding an entry. Explicit fragments take precedence; unknown values and back/forward restoration remain untouched. `#atomic-structure`, `#interactive-device` and `#interactive-stack` remain valid Lab aliases. Existing fragment IDs on Device, Why MoS₂ and the interface article remain at the corresponding static explanations; a link that previously named an interactive section does not silently jump to another route.

Viewers mount within 200px of the viewport and remain mounted until leaving a wider 900px boundary, avoiding repeated initialization from small scrolls. Far-away viewers release their renderer while their module controls and camera state remain mounted. Static previews preserve the document layout. Rendering is on demand; offscreen or hidden-document transitions pause. Cleanup releases renderer resources, animation frames, animations, observers and listeners. This is viewport-based residency, not a promised fixed maximum of WebGL contexts. Failure to load WebGL keeps a labeled static fallback and scientific explanations available. See [the architecture record](docs/unified-3d-lab.md) for implementation ownership and verification scope.

Device, Why MoS₂ and the interface article retain static scientific diagrams, complete theoretical explanations, citations and existing non-3D activities. Device retains its experimental micrograph and component roles. The interface article retains the attributed EOT plot, measurements, primary source and limits. Original conceptual diagrams remain explicitly distinct from experimental images and data.

## Preserved flows

- Shared navigation and scroll rules come from the owner’s 14 October 2026 content/navigation/UX brief; the existing route set and research parameter values define compatibility.
- Navigation links remain real links. Mobile menu opens with a button, closes on link selection and Escape, and returns focus on Escape. Page and research deep links continue to resolve.
- The five peer categories include Engineering Challenges. The mobile menu owns vertical overflow on short screens. Prose citations remain attached to their sentence; linked source actions and their citations wrap as a group.
- The homepage's decorative silver particles drift continuously in normal motion mode. The owner's latest instruction removes the pause control. System reduced motion freezes the scene and hidden/offscreen rendering pauses without resetting its phase.
- Foundations retains all six science topics, sliders, selectors, Fermi/transport activities, disclosures and the FET state toggle. Material controls keep their native button/keyboard behavior.
- The nine nanoscale length examples, logarithmic slider and live preview share one selection; each selected example names its measurement type and source. Carbon lengths are identified as bonds, lattice constants or layer spacing, rather than atomic diameters. Example cards expose their pressed state; the range exposes its physical size in nanometres or micrometres. A custom slider value clears the example selection.
- Transistor materials and their citations remain visible in the inspector. Full role explanations and model limits use native disclosures; model limits span the shared workbench. Published simulator evidence retains the complete original image beside its caption and provenance, stacked on phones.
- Lab retains orbit/zoom/reset/focus, selection, visibility, assembly/explosion and local scientific controls in four independently stateful modules. Keyboard arrows and camera buttons provide alternatives to dragging; touch orbit is opt-in so page scrolling remains available. Full yaw and above/below pitch work in the shared scientific viewers. Graphene detail buttons select a sheet, single C–C bond or local three-neighbour motif. The separate MoS₂ module retains monolayer/bilayer, 2H/3R registry, atomic-plane inspection, spacing and primitive-cell controls. The general transistor retains conceptual ON/OFF; interface engineering retains with/without-carbon-dot comparison. Material examples, geometry citations and model limits stay beside the corresponding model.
- Simulator input changes recalculate immediately. Existing normalization and five parameter meanings do not change. Save records a bounded versioned scenario in browser-local storage; errors remain visible. Reset restores the existing defaults.
- Comparison retains A/B selection, shared scales, overlay/side-by-side layouts, parameter/readout table, edit links and saved-scenario removal. Removal retains the existing immediate behavior under the user's explicit requirement to preserve original functions; this redesign does not invent a new destructive server operation.
- Each of the five parent categories retains its direct route. 3D Lab is a direct link without a model submenu or expand button on desktop and mobile; the other four categories expose two groups of actual pages/sections. Dropdown Overview entries remain removed. Hover and a separate expand button open one submenu; hidden panels are inert. Keyboard ArrowDown, Tab and Escape have explicit destinations. Mobile expands subgroups inside its scrollable menu.
- Explicit cross-route links without a fragment start at the new page top immediately. Explicit fragment links reveal collapsed ancestors and position below the header. Same-page anchors scroll smoothly except with reduced motion. Popstate cancels pending corrections and preserves history restoration. Query-only controls and synthetic hash state notifications do not reset scrolling.
- Research filter/selection state remains in the URL; clear restores the full study set. Unmatched filters show recovery guidance. Study disclosures and timeline links remain available. Catalogue summaries are grouped under the five existing research-category values, each in a responsive grid, and start collapsed unless selected by a query/fragment; an expanded entry spans its category grid width and exposes every existing evidence/analysis field. Its control identifies the paper and switches between View and Close study analysis. Timeline selection intentionally opens and scrolls to the study. Numbered references remain visible outside the disclosure. DOI, authors and publication access are centralized in ReferenceBrowser, with ReferenceAccess retaining only genuinely distinct resources.
- Reference search is local and immediate. Clear returns focus to search. Citation selection and safe return links remain intact. The finite bibliography is rendered in full rather than paginated.

## State and access

Public content and mathematical experiments require no account or remote data mutation. Local-storage failure must not block calculation; recorded scenarios remain local to this browser. The website has no billing, server CRUD, sessions or permission flow. Error, loading and not-found routes retain navigation. Reduced-motion, keyboard focus and mobile alternatives apply throughout. English is the sole public UI language in this change.
