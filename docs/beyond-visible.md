## 3D and interaction contracts

- Geometry: nearest-neighbour bond invariants, no covalent interlayer bonds, one S–Mo–S sheet per monolayer, primitive-cell boundary rather than a mistaken six-atom basis.
- Quality: shared sphere/cylinder/bevel geometry, instanced atoms/bonds, antialiasing, capped DPR, ACES/SRGB, procedural environment and key/rim lighting. Source images are not used as 3D textures.
- Anime.js 4: cancellable assembly reveal, layer/sheet inspection transitions, device explosion and camera focus/orbit-button transitions. These are presentation transitions, not molecular dynamics or quantum trajectories.
- Controls: pointer orbit, zoom, keyboard/buttons/reset, selection and visibility, labels, cell overlay, stacking, monolayer/bilayer and optical/transport learning links. One-finger touch orbit is opt-in; ordinary vertical scrolling is available.
- Lifecycle: viewport-deferred import, demand rendering, offscreen/hidden pause, reduced-motion finalization, listener/observer/animation/material/geometry/environment/context cleanup.
- Failure: native descriptions and static schematic when bundle/WebGL/context fails. No fake scientific performance curve appears during a loading/error state.
