# Particle and 3D refinement — 13 October 2026

The homepage now projects a continuously twisting silver particle volume. Its surface brightness and perspective suggest depth; this is decorative art, not a molecular dynamics calculation. The owner requested uninterrupted motion without a visible pause control. System reduced motion and hidden/offscreen suspension remain. The three capability links lead to the atomic lab, device and research pages.

Graphene close-ups are subsets of the same ideal lattice: one neighbouring C–C pair and an interior carbon with three neighbours. Their approximately 1.42 Å spacing and 120° planar angles are preserved. These are local sheet fragments, not a free C₂ molecule or relaxed molecular cluster. Shared orbit controls allow full yaw and underside inspection. The lab presents the shared transistor inline; /device and the interface discovery reuse that renderer.
