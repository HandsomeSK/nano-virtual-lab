# Reference and image-source verification — 21 October 2026

The scientific revision reviewed every one of the 36 pre-existing bibliography entries, then appended 15 sources needed by the new characterization, optics, fabrication and application explanations. Existing IDs and numbers 1–36 are retained. All 51 IDs and all 46 DOI values are unique.

## Method and limits

Publisher or primary-author records establish title, author attribution, journal/year and DOI. Public abstracts support only their stated methods/results. Full-text access is identified below where available; an accessible landing page, abstract, correction notice or search-indexed publisher passage does not establish inspection of paywalled methods or supplementary datasets. Team-specific numerical/structural checks are also recorded in the scientific, atomic and simulation audit documents. Direct Crossref API requests were blocked by the executor proxy (HTTP 403), so this is not described as a Crossref API validation. No record was rejected as fabricated; the corrections concern scope, access precision and bibliographic completeness.

The review date describes the selected records actually checked, not comprehensive coverage of all current MoS₂ research. Old notes about earlier checks remain historical records unless explicitly updated. No external figure was added.

### Corrections and publication chronology

- Qiu, da Jornada and Louie: the [2015 erratum](https://journals.aps.org/prl/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevLett.115.119901) was read in full. Finer convergence changes the stated quasiparticle gap and A-exciton binding energy. The original abstract’s near-1 eV binding-energy value is not used. The new teaching text explains the optical/quasiparticle distinction without asserting a universal numerical binding energy.
- Li et al.: the publisher links the [2016 corrigendum](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmat4564). Only the accessible qualitative vacancy/strain activation finding is used; uninspected correction details or catalyst performance values are not reconstructed.
- Buscema et al.: the journal volume/citation is 2014, while the migrated Springer page lists 7 January 2015 as its online date. The registry makes this visible rather than silently changing the journal year.
- George: the review appeared in the 2010 issue after online publication in November 2009. Publisher metadata was accessible; the full publisher review was not. The accessible MoS₂ interface study supplies the material-specific ALD case.
